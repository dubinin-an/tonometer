# Tonometer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Tonometer is an offline-first blood pressure journal. The application does not require an account, display advertising, include analytics, or send measurement data to the developer.

## Data stored on the device

Blood pressure and pulse measurements, comments, reminder settings, and custom tonometer profiles are stored locally on the user's device. The data remains there until the user deletes it or removes the application.

## Camera and photographs

Camera access is used to photograph and recognize a blood pressure monitor display. Recognition and image processing take place on the device. Photographs are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

## Notifications and alarms

Notification, boot-completed, and alarm permissions are used only to schedule measurement reminders selected by the user and to restore those reminders after a device restart or application update.

## Export and backup

Measurement CSV files and tonometer-profile backups leave the application only when the user explicitly invokes export or sharing. Android then lets the user choose the receiving application or storage location. That recipient's privacy practices apply after the export.

## Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through Tonometer. The application has no developer-operated backend service.

## Data security, retention, and deletion

Application data is kept in the app's private storage and is not transmitted to the developer. The user can delete individual measurements, series, and custom profiles in the application. Removing the application deletes its locally stored application data, subject to Android backup settings controlled by the user and device.

## Medical disclaimer

Tonometer is a personal journal and recognition aid, not a medical device. Recognition results can be incorrect and must be checked by the user. The application does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical decisions.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in an application update and on the public policy page.
