# Google Play listing draft

## English (en-US)

**App name:** Tonometer

**Short description:** Record blood pressure from a monitor photo or enter it manually.

**Full description:**

Tonometer is a private, offline-first journal for blood pressure and pulse readings.

- Photograph a compatible monitor display and check the recognized values before saving.
- Enter or correct SYS, DIA, pulse, date, time, and measurement arm.
- Record two or three readings as a series and keep the average together.
- Review daily averages and trends on three clear charts.
- Set local reminders for regular measurements.
- Export measurements to CSV and back up custom monitor profiles.
- Keep data on the device: no account, ads, analytics, or developer-operated cloud.

Recognition can make mistakes. Always compare saved values with the monitor display. Tonometer is a personal journal and recognition aid, not a medical device, and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical decisions.
